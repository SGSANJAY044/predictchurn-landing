The problem

By the time they click cancel, the decision is weeks old.

Billing still looks fine. The card still goes through. Meanwhile usage is fading, seats are dropping, and nobody on the team owns the account. The exit survey then gives you the politest possible answer.

How it works

Follow one account from quiet to kept.

Week 1

Everything looks healthy

Acme Freight runs its weekly report like clockwork. PredictChurn learns what normal looks like for this account.

Week 5

The account drifts from its own normal

The report stops and seats drop. Stripe still shows a paying customer, so no other dashboard reacts.

Week 6

Your team sees why, and who owns it

It's flagged with the evidence, weighted for its $2,400 plan, and assigned to Alex on the CS team.

Week 7

Alex approves the next move

A check-in email and call link are drafted. Nothing goes out until a person approves it.

Week 9

The account comes back

Usage recovers before renewal. The whole team can see what happened and what worked.

Questions

Fair things to ask.

Is PredictChurn live?

Not yet. v1 is in development and waitlist teams get access first.

How is this different from other churn tools?

Many churn tools focus on the cancel button, failed payments, or a founder working alone. PredictChurn is built around a small CS team sharing one at-risk list, with owners, per-plan weights, and approvals.

Will it message our customers on its own?

No. Emails, Slack alerts, offers, and call invites all wait for someone on your team to approve them.

We don't use PostHog. Can we still join?

Yes. PostHog is the first usage source. Tell us what you use on the waitlist, because it helps decide what we support next.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. There's no card and no commitment.
